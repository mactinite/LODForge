What decides the pages (all checked against the reference `.lst`):
- **Which textures**: per reference and LOD model shape, the texture LODGen ends up with (AltTextures swap
  like for like, else the shape's own; a mini-atlas source becomes its mini atlas), plus the
  billboards the export places as `.dds` models and the ICLOD model. No UV range: a range of 4 and of
  10000 give the same list, shapes reaching u = 3.8 are on DynDOLOD's atlas. The list is
  DynDOLOD's `LODGen_SSE_TexturesUsed_<W>.txt` (`diffuse,alpha/16,normal,`, 380 lines = the map).
- **Which page**: texture-only. Any texel under alpha 128 → `_Alpha`, else the BC1 page: 288/288 shared
  keys (the shapes' NiAlphaProperty gets 10 wrong; "any texel < 255" gets 202 wrong, BC7 noise).
- **Tile size**: LOD textures (`\lod\`, `\lodgen\`) up to `ObjectLODMaxTileSize` 1024, full textures
  halved to `FullTextureMaxTileSize` 256 (2048² and 4096² → 256, 2048x4096 → 128x256); keys with an
  alpha threshold below 128 (`=4`, `=2`) get twice that.
- **Page size**: square bin as wide as the power of two covering √(tile area), doubled until all fit,
  cropped to the tiles (as the glow page).
- **Mips**: `ObjectLODMinMipMaps`/`TreeLODMinMipMaps` 8; alpha pages and the tree atlas fill
  see-through texels with the average colour (`*CreateAverageColor`) and keep each tile's coverage
  at alpha 128 in lower mips (`AlphaCoverage`; DynDOLOD's exact `AlphaFactor` formula is not known).

## 1. PBR (Community Shaders / PGPatcher / ParallaxGen)

What the PBR content looks like in this profile:
- PGPatcher Output: 2,858 patched full models (rocks, mountains, ice, glaciers, Markarth,
  caves). Example `landscape\mountains\mountaincliff01.nif`: lighting shader type 11, SLSF1
  0x82400301, SLSF2 0x00809031 (0x800000 is the Community Shaders True PBR bit), slots 0 diffuse
  `textures\pbr\...\mountainslab01.dds`, 1 `_n`, 3 `_p` (parallax/height), 5 `_rmaos`.
  PGPatcher also duplicates meshes under `meshes\_pgpatcher_dups\<n>\...` and points bases at
  them (7,394 Tamriel export lines use such a path).
- Faultier's PBR Landscapes 2k: `textures\PBR\landscape\*` albedo stored **BC1_UNORM_SRGB**,
  `_n` BC7, `_p` BC4, `_rmaos` BC1; plus `PBRTextureSets` / `PBRMaterialObjects` JSON (used by
  PGPatcher, not by the LOD tools).

What DynDOLOD does with it (all confirmed in the reference output):
- LOD shapes never get True PBR. Every LOD shape is a plain type-0 lighting shader
  (SLSF1 0x80000300, SLSF2 0x80000021 for HD snow), so DynDOLOD converts PBR to "classic" LOD
  instead of carrying CS PBR into LOD. LODGen 3.1.8 keeps PBR out of specular resets (type 1 or
  SLSF2 0x800000), which LODForge ports (`crates/objlod/src/shape.rs:171-237`).
- Where a LOD shape shows a PBR texture directly (HD mountains), the diffuse is a
  **`<name>linear.dds` copy** DynDOLOD writes: 47 files in Tamriel, BC7 UNORM at half the source
  size (`mountainslab01.dds` 8192² BC1_SRGB → `mountainslab01linear.dds` 4096² BC7). 1,748 of the
  4,729 LOD4 texture references point at `textures\pbr\` (mountainslab01/02linear, `_n`, and
  `mountainslab02_rmaos` in slot 5 on 122 shapes).
- Everything else maps PBR full textures onto TexGen's `pbr_lod` mini atlases (66 stitched +
  12 rendered PBR twins) through the atlas map.

  (`crates/dyndolod/src/lodmodels.rs:22-40`), same as DynDOLOD (reference export maps

  (The `<stem>_<CRC32>_lod` variants are a gap, see P7.)
- Terrain LOD with PBR landscape textures: xLODGen does no sRGB handling at all (no `pbr` /
  `srgb` strings in `xLODGenx64.exe`; docs/re/xlodgen-terrain-textures.md:171) and LODForge
  matches that. Not a parity gap. (An sRGB-aware bake would be an improvement over xLODGen, not
  parity.)
- NIF writer: can write every BSLightingShaderProperty type tail (1, 5, 6, 7, 11, 14, 16), the
  SSE BSEffectShaderProperty, texture sets of any length and NiAlphaProperty; no PBR gap is
  caused by the writer.

## 6. How close is LODForge to feature-complete?

For the default DynDOLOD workflow (Medium preset, seasons, TexGen, occlusion) on a vanilla-texture
load order, LODForge is close: every output type DynDOLOD writes exists, LOD geometry matches
(94/92/83% of triangles on LOD4/8/16, 391/744 BTT byte-identical, all 649 TexGen object textures
and 156 PBR twins present), and the remaining gaps there are fidelity bugs (G1, G3, G31, P4) and
option-gated features.

On a PBR load order (this profile) it is **not** close yet. DynDOLOD has a PBR→LOD conversion
layer that LODForge lacks almost entirely: per-reference PBR swaps (P1, in progress), `linear`
albedo copies (P2), PBR mini atlases (P3), PBR texture sets on dynamic LOD bases (P6), the
ParallaxGen CRC map (P7) and PBR-normalised HD masks (P4). Together they explain the grey/vanilla
mountains and rocks and most of the HD-shape deficit. All of it is S–M work with known formats and
> _(detail that cited the LODGen decompile or disassembly was moved to the quarantine folder on 2026-10-20; the status in section 0 stands)_

Beyond that, the largest pieces are: un-atlased full textures (G1, 3–4x the draw calls of
DynDOLOD), ultra / DynDOLOD-style tree LOD (G6, L), grass BTO billboard completeness (G32),
the unread config lists and rule sections (G16/G17), and the TexGen/preset settings that are
hardcoded (G22, G14). About 60 more INI keys are absent but only matter when a user changes them.

Rough count: 7 PBR gaps + 4 suspected, 37 general gaps (about 12 visible with default
settings) + 6 suspected, plus the already-known list in §4.
